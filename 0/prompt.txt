## Goal

Mirror MATLAB's inline suppression directive. A comment of the form `%#ok<RULE_ID>` on a line suppresses diagnostics for that rule on that line. Support forms:
- `%#ok<NOSEMI>` — suppress one rule
- `%#ok<*NOSEMI*>` — same (with optional surrounding `*`)
- `%#ok<*all*>` or `%#ok<*>...` — suppress ALL rules on the line
- `%#ok<NOSEMI,AGROW>` — comma-separated multiple rules
- `%#ok<*NOSEMI*>` — wildcard-matched prefix/suffix (match if the directive rule-id matches by prefix/suffix with `*`)

The directive applies only to the line it appears on (MATLAB semantics). Match against the diagnostic's `rule_id` (the specific check ID, e.g. "NOSEMI", not the engine meta-ID like "NOSEMI" engine or "SYNTAX_ERRORS_ENGINE").
